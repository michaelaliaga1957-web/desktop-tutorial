**Price/quote (enough info given):**

everything. No payment now; you pay after once it's done and you're happy. I'll send a
confirmation. Anything you want focused on, just say the word.

## 7. Lead-response decision tree
NEW LEAD →
  Did they give enough to quote (vehicle + service)?
    YES → Price/quote reply + propose a day/time. Ask ONLY make/model if missing.
    NO  → Ask only the ONE missing detail (exterior/interior/full?). Never make them repeat.
  Interested? →
    YES → Booking-closer (lock day, time, address).
    NO REPLY (4–6 hrs) → "Following up — I have a [DAY/TIME] window open, want me to hold it?"
    STILL NO REPLY (next day) → one final: "Still happy to help whenever — just say the word."
Keep it ON Thumbtack until booked (protects the lead-fee refund). Swap numbers AFTER booking.

## 8. Offer (controlled, margin-safe)
- **First-time:** $25 off first SuperWash (already live). Keep.
- **Weekday-fill:** "Book Mon–Thu this week and I'll add a 1-year sealant free" (sealant is
  high-margin; fills dead weekday slots without discounting the base price).
- **Two-vehicle:** "$20 off when you do 2 vehicles same visit" (efficiency = your gas/time
  are already spent on-site).
- **Referral:** both parties $15 off (win-back text above).
All have a limit/expiration, are easy to stop, and protect the base price.

## 9. Content system (every completed job → assets)
Per job, capture: 1 before, 1 after (same angle), 1 action shot. Then:
- GBP post (before/after + "booked in [neighborhood] today")
- Instagram post + story
- Reuse best after-shot as future ad creative + Thumbtack/website photo
Ask for the Google review at the car, while they're looking at the result (highest yes-rate).

## 10. Decision rules
- Thumbtack lead cost creeping up with low close rate → tighten targeting / respond faster, don't raise budget.
- A channel produces 3+ bookings → document exactly what worked, repeat it.
- A partnership sends recurring work → double down, add more shops like it.
- Google Ads: fix conversion tracking + delivery BEFORE adding budget. Keep at $2–3/day until the funnel converts.
- TEST → MEASURE → keep winners → kill losers → scale winners.
