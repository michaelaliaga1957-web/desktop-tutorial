## Thumbtack lead texts — READ THIS BEFORE DRAFTING ANY LEAD MESSAGE

When the owner asks for a text to a Thumbtack/OfferUp lead or customer, follow the
approved model below. Full reference in **`leads-playbook.md`**. Essentials are inlined
here so they're always in context, even on a lighter model. Don't improvise a different
style.

### Output rules
- Output the text message and nothing else (a one-line "Ready to send?" after is fine).
- **2 to 4 short lines. Never a paragraph.** If it's long, cut it.
- Sound like a confident real person texting — not a brochure, not corporate.

### The shape of a first text
1. `Hey [Name]!` (or `Quick heads up, [Name] —` for a same-day freed-up slot)
2. `I can do a full exterior and interior detail on your [vehicle] — $[price].`
3. `Got an opening [today/tomorrow] around [time]?`
4. `Just need your address to lock it in.`

### Hard rules
- **NEVER** use the word "penciled."
- **Answer their question FIRST**, then offer a time.
- **Don't invent an opening when you don't know what they need yet** — ask first.
- Never undercut price to win — sell on value (mobile, no drop-off, stand behind the
  work). Offer, don't beg.
- Keep it short. One or two ideas per text, max.

### Pricing (exact)
- Car / sedan / crossover full detail: **$164.99** (first-time rate)
- Truck / SUV full detail: **$189.99**
- Moto detail: **$124.99**
- Basic 3-month wax: **included** with every detail; 1-year wax: add-on
- Light shampoo standard with interior; "shampoo focus" is a $49.99 add-on
- Odor / Deep Steam Refresh: ~$39.99 add-on · Pet hair removal: add-on
- Heavy condition / far-out location / rush: adjust up (travel fee, condition charge)

### Copy-paste templates
Standard: `Hey [Name]! I can do a full exterior and interior detail on your [vehicle] — $[price]. Got an opening [today/tomorrow] around [time]? Just need your address to lock it in.`

Same-day heads up: `Quick heads up, [Name] — I'm wrapping up a detail right now and a [time] spot just freed up today. I could head straight to you after. Want it? Just need your address.`

Vague lead (ask first, no time yet): `Hey [Name]! Thanks for reaching out. I do full exterior and interior detailing for your [vehicle]. What are you looking to get done — just a detail, or anything specific like wax protection or interior treatments?`

Wax question: `The basic 3-month wax is included with the detail. If you want the 1-year paint protection instead, let me know and we can adjust.`

### Before sending, check
Name used? · 2–4 short lines? · Answered their question first? · Right price for the
vehicle? · Asked for the address/next step? · Zero "penciled"? · Sounds human?

## THUMBTACK REFUND POLICY (lead fees) — quick reference

Refunds, when approved, go to your **Thumbtack balance** (credit for future leads), not
your card. Full detail in `leads-playbook.md`.

- **Auto-refund:** if the customer **doesn't respond to ANY pro within 72 hours**,
  Thumbtack refunds the lead automatically.
- **NOT refundable if:** the customer **replied to at least one pro**, didn't hire you,
  or you just think the **price was too high**.
- **Valid refund-request reasons:** Location (not in service area) · Timing (outside
  availability) · Job type (not my line of work) · Charged twice (duplicate) · Phone
  disconnected/out of service · Other.
- **Stay eligible:** respond fast, keep ALL communication on Thumbtack, and "Report" a
  lead if it looks like spam.
- **Rule of thumb:** customer replied to anyone → generally NOT refundable, even if they
  ghost or pick someone else afterward.
