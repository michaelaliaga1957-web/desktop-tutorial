Common questions.Preguntas frecuentes.

Do you need water and power at my location?¿Necesitan agua y corriente?

No. We are 100% self-contained — we bring our own water tank, generator, and all professional equipment. You don't need to provide anything but the vehicle. No. Somos 100% autosuficientes — traemos nuestro tanque de agua, generador y todo el equipo profesional. Solo necesitas el vehículo.

How long does a detail take?¿Cuánto tarda un detallado?

Exterior Focused: 1–2 hours. Interior Focused: 1.5–2.5 hours. SuperWash (full detail): 2.5–4 hours depending on vehicle size and condition. Exterior: 1–2 horas. Interior: 1.5–2.5 horas. SuperWash: 2.5–4 horas según tamaño y condición del vehículo.

Do I need to be present during the detail?¿Debo estar presente?

No. Just make sure we have access to your vehicle. We text you when we arrive and when we're done so you can inspect the results. No. Solo asegúrate de que tengamos acceso al vehículo. Te avisamos al llegar y al terminar para que inspecciones.

What areas do you serve in Houston?¿Qué zonas cubren?

All of Houston and surrounding areas including Katy, Sugar Land, Pearland, The Woodlands, Cypress, and Spring. Most of Houston is covered free. For the outer edges of our area, a small travel fee keeps us coming to you, always confirmed before booking — never a surprise. Todo Houston y alrededores incluyendo Katy, Sugar Land, Pearland, The Woodlands, Cypress y Spring. La mayor parte sin cargo. En zonas lejanas aplica una tarifa pequeña, siempre confirmada antes de reservar.

What if it rains on my appointment day?¿Y si llueve?

If there's heavy rain we'll reschedule at no charge. Light rain is usually fine for interior-only services. Si hay lluvia fuerte reprogramamos sin costo. La lluvia ligera generalmente está bien para servicios interiores.

How and when do I pay?¿Cómo y cuándo pago?

Payment is collected after the service is completed and you've inspected the work. We accept credit/debit cards, Zelle, Venmo, and cash. No payment is required to book. El pago se realiza después del servicio y de que inspecciones el trabajo. Aceptamos tarjeta, Zelle, Venmo y efectivo. No se requiere pago para reservar.

Do you offer recurring or subscription detailing?¿Ofrecen servicio recurrente?

Yes. Ask about our monthly maintenance packages when you book — regular clients get priority scheduling and discounted rates. Sí. Pregunta por nuestros paquetes mensuales al reservar — los clientes regulares reciben prioridad y tarifas con descuento.

Is the trunk included?¿Incluye la cajuela?

Trunk cleaning is offered by special request. Just mention it when you book and we'll include it in your service. La limpieza de cajuela se ofrece por petición especial. Menciónalo al reservar y lo incluimos.
